# Cinematic Statement Section

## Component

`src/sections/Statement.tsx`

## Purpose

This pinned section acts as a pacing reset between the hero and the deeper operational content. It gives the site a memorable line without adding visual clutter.

## Current Copy

`Luxury is the absence of exposed effort.`

## Motion

- GSAP pins the section briefly.
- Quote fades and sharpens into view.
- Gold rule expands.
- Quote fades out as the user exits the section.

## Handoff Checks

- Confirm the section scrolls smoothly on desktop and mobile.
- If the quote changes, keep it short enough to avoid mobile overflow.
